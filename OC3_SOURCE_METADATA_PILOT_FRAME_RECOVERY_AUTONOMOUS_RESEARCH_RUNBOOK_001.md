# Pilot-Frame Recovery Autonomous Research Runbook 001

1. Verify branch, predecessor STOP artifacts, Policy Core, mandate, waiting state, first candidate and receipt.
2. Obtain separate standing human authorization bound to exact waiting-state and candidate hashes; bootstrap creates none.
3. Activate, register, evaluate and issue one new frame-recovery permit. Consume it before launching the worker.
4. Supervisor creates output/intent, launches the exact worker once, bounds and fingerprints both streams, records child status/resources, validates the frame and writes diagnostic and terminal.
5. Transition with zero request/body/retry deltas. A valid frame closes `RECOVERED`; input/invariant violation closes `INTEGRITY_FAILED`; environment or runtime inability closes `INCONCLUSIVE` or governed STOP as contracted.
6. Finalize through the governor. Never continue to Data Lab, counts, rows, topology or matching.

A consumed permit is never replayed. Preserve partial evidence and require human review after an uncontracted interruption.
