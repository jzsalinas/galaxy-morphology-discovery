# OC3 Observational Multiplicity Autonomy Policy Core Contract 001

## Purpose

This mission policy core decides only whether an epistemically material action remains inside the frozen observational-multiplicity mandate. An action-specific validator separately proves the candidate, exact command, implementation, frozen specification, literal resources, and action firewalls.

The universal contract is `OC3_OBSERVATIONAL_MULTIPLICITY_ACTION_CONTRACT_002`. It accepts different offline, documentary, and prospectively contracted grouping actions without changing the policy core. Every candidate binds its payload, externally bound full-file identity, command, validation receipt, implementation, frozen specification, authority subset, resource manifest when networked, reservations, concurrency, retry and resume policies, zero scientific firewall, empty requested prohibited scopes, Git assertions, permit path, and mandate.

## Frozen envelope

Eligible authority classes are exactly those in the mandate. Any nonempty subset is eligible in principle. Network actions require a sealed literal resource manifest and must fit the remaining request and body budgets. Concurrency is at most one. A retry is eligible only when prospectively frozen for the exact same resource. Offline actions reserve zero network resources and may omit a resource manifest.

PHOTSYS, Tractor/source rows, pixels, morphology, labels, models, embeddings, and clustering remain at zero. Grouping values require their own prospective specification and action-specific validator. Requested prohibited scopes must be the empty list.

## Lifecycle

Activation is one-shot and binds the exact waiting state, standing authorization, mandate, policy manifest, first candidate, and branch. Registration is generic and consumes no budget. Permits derive from the candidate contract and are immutable at the candidate-declared path. Consumption identity is the permit SHA under `oc3/OBSERVATIONAL_MULTIPLICITY_AUTONOMY_LEDGER/PERMIT_CONSUMPTION/`; no caller-selected alternate path participates in replay protection.

A completion transition revalidates the policy core, authorization, active state, candidate, permit, canonical consumption marker, actual terminal SHA, exposed terminal counters, reservations, and remaining mission budgets. A consumed permit without a valid terminal cannot be replayed; absent a prospectively frozen restart rule, the mission requires `STOP_REQUIRES_HUMAN`.

State transitions snapshot the previous state, append sealed compact ledger evidence, increment sequence, and atomically replace state. STOP and the four scientific terminals set `active=false`. Runtime-heavy evidence remains outside Git.

## Immutability

The policy-core manifest binds this contract and both governor implementation files by path and SHA-256. A future standing authorization must bind that exact manifest. Once active, any member mismatch yields `POLICY_CORE_MISMATCH` and requires `STOP_REQUIRES_HUMAN`. New action-specific specifications, validators, receipts, and candidates may be added inside the mandate; the universal governor may not be changed to make an action eligible.
