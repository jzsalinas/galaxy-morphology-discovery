## Prospective governance substitution

An eligible prospective stage registered under an active standing mandate may use a canonical, sealed, single-use `AUTONOMOUS_EXECUTION_PERMIT` instead of a per-stage `FINAL_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION`. The truthful authority chain is human reviewer → standing human autonomy authorization → deterministic policy evaluation → action-specific autonomous execution permit → execution evidence.

Historical `FINAL_HUMAN_AUTHORIZATION` artifacts retain their original meaning and cannot be reused, converted, or represented as autonomous permits. Historical candidates remain governed by their frozen schemas.

The deterministic governor decides only whether a proposed action is inside the active mandate. It does not decide scientific truth. A permit binds the active mandate and standing authorization, the exact pre-action state, sequence, stage, scope, candidate, command, resource manifest, reserved budgets, firewall, resume rule, and issuance time. Permits are single-use; copied or consumed permits cannot authorize replay.

## Fail-closed boundary

No active standing authorization means no permit. Candidate, command, state, manifest, budget, authority-class, branch, firewall, or sequence mismatch means no permit. Actions outside the mandate enter `STOP_REQUIRES_HUMAN`; they do not broaden the envelope automatically.

The standing mandate cannot authorize Panel V2, P1, a V2 resolver, new astronomical observations, real PHOTSYS/BRICKNAME/BRICKID/ROOT value access, criteria rewritten after evidence, destructive evidence handling, credentials, force push, history rewrite, or authority-class expansion.
